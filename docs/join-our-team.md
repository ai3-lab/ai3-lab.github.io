# Join Our Team

We are always looking for motivated researchers to join our laboratory. If you are interested in spatial omics research and computational biology, please contact us to discuss potential opportunities.

### How to Apply

For inquiries about joining our laboratory, please send your CV and a brief cover letter describing your research interests and career goals to [yeongps@a-star.edu.sg](mailto:yeongps@a-star.edu.sg).

### What We Look For

- Strong background in biology, computational science, or a related field
- Interest in spatial omics, cancer immunology, or AI-driven pathology
- Collaborative mindset and willingness to work in a multidisciplinary team
- For PhD candidates: a relevant Master's degree or equivalent research experience

### Why Join Us

- Work at the forefront of spatial omics and cancer immunotherapy research
- Access to state-of-the-art research platforms and computational resources
- Collaborative environment spanning A*STAR and Singapore General Hospital
- Mentorship from a world-recognized leader in the field (Top 2% Scientist)
- Opportunities for international collaboration and conference participation
